In this short note I cover how to orchestrate a hero animation that plays once: staggered text, a typed subtitle, and a long, slow gradient arc.

Key ideas:

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and fall back to static UI.
- Keep durations short for interactions, long for ambient.
- Avoid layout shift: reserve space and animate transforms/opacity.
