## Overview

Players choose a number from 0–999 and submit using a commit–reveal scheme to prevent front‑running. Rounds settle against on-chain finality and 70% of deposits pay out to winners.

Entries cost **5 USDC** and multiple entries are supported.

## Design Notes

- Commit–reveal to secure user guesses
- Deterministic payout split and secure claim flow
- Transparent randomness via on-chain finality
- Clear round lifecycle and stakes
