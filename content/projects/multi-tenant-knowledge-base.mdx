## Description

A lightweight multi-tenant knowledge-base backend built with TypeScript, Node.js, and Express, featuring CRUD for articles, filtering, and event-driven duplicate detection.

### Overview

This service manages knowledge articles for multiple tenants. It supports:
- Tenant & article management
- Aliases and optional topics
- Filtering by title/alias, tenant, and publication year
- Atomic article updates
- Event-driven duplicate detection (title or alias matches)
