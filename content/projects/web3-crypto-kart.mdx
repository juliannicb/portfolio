## Overview

Karts and tracks are minted as NFTs. Players enter tournaments using crypto; an on-chain payout model distributes rewards to winners and track owners.

The frontend renders a canvas-based race animation and integrates wallet-ready flows for entry and settlement.

## Highlights

- NFT assets for karts and tracks
- Tournament entry in crypto
- Simple, auditable payout mechanics
- Canvas animation front-end
